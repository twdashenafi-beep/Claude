Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 September 2026

DayFlow cannot read your tasks. Not because we promise not to, but because everything is encrypted on your device with a key derived from a password we never receive.

What is collected

DataWhyCan we read it?
Email addressTo identify your account and let your devices syncYes
Task contentTo sync between your devicesNo — stored only as ciphertext
TimestampsTo decide which edit wins when two devices disagreeYes

That is the complete list. There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDK collecting anything, and no profile built about you.

How the encryption works

Your tasks are encrypted with AES-256 using a key that never leaves your device. Your password is put through PBKDF2-SHA256 (210,000 iterations, your email as the salt) to produce two separate values: an authentication hash, which is the only thing the server receives, and a key-encrypting key, which stays with you.

Because the authentication hash is a one-way function of your key material, the server cannot work backwards from it to anything that decrypts your data. A full database dump would reveal how many tasks you have and when they last changed — not one word of what they say.

What we cannot do

We cannot reset your password, read your tasks, recover your data if you lose both your password and your recovery code, or hand your task content to anyone who asks — including in response to a legal demand, because we do not hold the key. This is a consequence of the design, not a policy that could change.

Where data is stored

Task ciphertext and your email address are held by Supabase, which provides the database and authentication. A copy of your encrypted tasks is also kept on each of your devices so the app works offline.

Your rights

You can delete your account and everything in it at any time from Account → Delete account inside the app. Deletion is immediate and permanent: the tasks, the wrapped key and the account record are all removed, from the server and from the device. There is no recovery window, and no backup we could restore from.

Because we hold nothing readable, a request to export or correct your task data is something only you can carry out — from within the app, where the key is.

Children

DayFlow is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date above changes and the app will say so before the change takes effect.
